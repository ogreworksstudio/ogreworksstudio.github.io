第一話Series · Battle shonen · In production

Rairaku
Hatoru

The thunder no power can silence.

The story

In a world where everyone channels the Pulse of a living Tower, Rairaku was born deaf to it: no power at all. Betrayed by his own brother and thrown into the Tower's deadliest prison, he returns seven years later as no one expected: pure muscle, technique and fury.

Why it works

In a genre full of power systems, the hero is the only one unplugged from them: raw strength against everything. The appeal of Mashle and Saitama, with the weight of Baki.

Tone

Baki · Kengan Ashura · Vinland Saga · Mashle. Black & white, heavy anatomy, impact panels.

Where it is

  • 18 chapters scripted: Arc 1 complete, Arc 2 at chapter 18 of 20
  • Chapter 1 being drawn by Armani Piazzalunga (@magyarskorpio127)
  • Registered work (Brazilian National Library)
  • A new chapter every week
ドン！乱哭RanKu, “the lament of chaos”: Rairaku's signature punch. No Pulse, no magic. A whole body turning into one blow.

第二話One-shot · Dark fantasy · 36 pages

Salt OgreOGRO DE SAL鬼は外！

Every year, he throws salt at his door and shouts “Ogres out!”… because nobody in town knows he's one.

The world

Once a year, in every kingdom, people salt their doorsteps and shout “Ogres out!” Salt burns every race that isn't human: that's how humans, the weakest race, came to rule the world. The ogres were thrown out through the great Gate.

The story

Jano was found as a baby at a human woman's door. She raised him in secret and taught him to survive: throw the salt, shout louder than everyone, and never let his strength out. Then, on the Day of Salt, something crawls out of the sea.

The look

Huge, heavy bodies in ink: think Baki's mass and Berserk's darkness. Black & white, right to left.

The script

  • 36 pages, complete: page beats + suggested panels
  • In English and Portuguese
  • Art: Gamafeu (@gamafeu)
  • A 50/50 partnership between writer and artist
  • Status: pre-production (art starts after the SMA entry)

The Sixtieth
Spring

Sixty springs. Two rivals. One cherry tree.

The story

Every first day of spring, for sixty years, two old rivals have climbed 300 stone steps to duel under a cherry tree: the first to cut the other's hat cord wins. This year, the rival is too fast to be an old man…

Why it fits the theme

“A Kind Heart” means thinking of the other first, even when it costs you. In this story, everyone is kind, and nobody says a word.

The entry

  • 16 pages, no dialogue: faces, hands and paneling tell everything
  • Silent Manga Audition: entries Nov 15, 2026 to Jan 15, 2027
  • Team entry with artist Gamafeu (@gamafeu): 50/50 on any prize, full art credit
  • Every entry gets feedback from a pro editor
  • Also written as an 8-page version with dialogue

How I Work

01

The story is mine

The worlds, the characters and every story choice are mine. Before a page is written, I decide what it is for and which moment should hit hardest.

02

The script is ready to draw

Page by page: the beat (what the page does) and suggested panels, with every line inside its panel. The scripts are drafted with an AI assistant; I choose, cut and edit every line.

03

The art is 100% yours

Drawn by hand, in your style. Paneling, angles and acting are your call: my panels are only suggestions. Your name goes on the cover.

What you get from me

  • A finished script before you draw a single page.
  • A 3-page script sample before you decide: just ask, and I send it by DM.
  • Rough thumbnails (a name) on request, so you can see what I imagined.
  • Clear answers, in English or Portuguese.
  • Proof of date for every story before it's shared.
  • A writer who keeps a rhythm: one new chapter of my series every week.
50/50 · No money upfront · Quality over speed

Working together

  1. 50/50 on everything the work earns.
  2. A permanent “Art by” credit.
  3. A simple written agreement before the first page.
  4. No money upfront, on either side.

Artists ask me

Do I get paid upfront?

No, and neither do I. Everything the story earns (prizes, publication, rights) is split 50/50 between us. That's why every project starts small, with a short story.

Do I keep my credit and my style?

Always. "Art by" with your name on the cover, forever. Paneling, angles and acting are your call: my panels are only suggestions.

Who writes the scripts? Is AI involved?

The worlds, the characters and every story decision are mine. The dialogue is drafted with an AI assistant, and I choose, cut and edit every line. The art is 100% yours, drawn by hand. I tell every artist this before anything else, so you can decide with all the facts.

Can I read a script before deciding?

Yes. Ask me and I'll send you a sample by DM. Unpublished scripts never go public: it protects the stories, and the contests they're entered in.

How fast do I need to draw?

At your pace. I write one chapter of my series every week, but the art follows your rhythm. Quality over speed.

Is there a contract?

Yes: a short written agreement before the first page, with the 50/50 split, the credit, and what happens if one of us has to stop.

What languages do you work in?

English or Portuguese. Scripts can also be prepared in French.
